Mirlo is a fair, artist-friendly platform for buying and streaming music from independent artists around the world.

Listen to a public catalog of music from independent musicians, and buy the albums and tracks you love directly. Mirlo is run as a cooperative and takes only a small platform fee, so more of what you pay goes to the artists who made the music.

What you can do with Mirlo:
- Stream the public catalog of independent music
- Buy albums and tracks that you own and keep
- Build your personal collection and wishlist
- Enjoy background playback with full playback controls
- Support artists directly on a platform built as a cooperative

Mirlo is committed to a fair, sustainable model for musicians and listeners alike. Purchases are completed securely through the web. Learn more at https://mirlo.space.
